## The experience we are building

**Create freely.** F11 should match the actual game CAS room in visual and interaction quality, enhanced by better browsing, exact values, safe copying and diagnostics.

**Keep every form yours.** Human and occult appearances must remain independently editable and survive switching, CAS, travel and save/reload. Special states need explicit capabilities rather than misleading one-size-fits-all controls.

**Know your content.** See the actual CAS image, item/swatch identity, installed package and dependencies. Understand a slider or conflict without leaving the editor.

**Experiment safely.** Preview a scoped change, see its diff, apply it transactionally, and use Undo/Redo. Preserve unrelated Sims, forms, outfits and custom colors.

**Stay fast.** No hidden polling or constant Sim scans by default. Optimize startup, indexing and frame times without content caps, fidelity reduction or compatibility regression.
